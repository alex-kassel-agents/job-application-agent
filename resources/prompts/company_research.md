# Protocol: Company Research & Pain Point Extraction

## Objective
Extract key business facts, corporate culture, core products, and operational challenges from the employer's website or job listing.

## Research Steps
1. **Core Business & Industry**: What does the company produce or provide? Who are their primary customers (B2B / B2C)?
2. **Current Challenges & Pain Points**: Why are they hiring? Are they expanding, modernizing ERP systems, introducing digital workflows, or handling increased regulatory compliance?
3. **Tech Stack & Tooling**: Specific software (Sage, SAP, SQL databases, cloud platforms, e-commerce systems).
4. **Tone & Culture**: Corporate, formal, innovative, traditional medium-sized enterprise (Mittelstand).

## Output Format (`company_research.md`)
Save the structured findings in the application directory:
```markdown
# Company Research: [Company Name]

## 1. Overview & Profile
- **Industry & Focus**: ...
- **Target Market**: ...

## 2. Identified Pain Points & Opportunity
- **Operational Need**: ...
- **Key Relevance to Candidate**: ...

## 3. Recommended Arguments for Cover Letter
- ...
```
